3. Plans, free trial and payment

Plans

  • Free plan. No time limit. It includes pickup orders and up to 50 products, plus the other features listed for Free on our Pricing page.
  • Premium. Adds delivery, table ordering, unlimited products and the other Premium features on our Pricing page. It is billed monthly, in US dollars unless your invoice says otherwise, per branch, at the price shown on our Pricing page.
  • Each branch has its own plan. Plans and limits can change as we improve the Services, and the Pricing page shows the current ones.

Free trial

  • New accounts can try Premium free for 30 days. No card is needed, and we won’t charge you for the trial.
  • We may limit the trial to one per business or branch.
  • When the trial ends, the branch moves to the Free plan unless you’ve agreed with us to upgrade. Nothing is deleted. Products above the Free limit of 50 are hidden from your storefront until you choose which 50 stay visible, and your Premium settings are kept and switch back on when you upgrade.

Upgrading and paying

  • For now, upgrades and new branches are arranged with our team. We’ll move this online when we can.
  • We invoice you, and you can pay by the methods we offer, such as cash, bank transfer, Whish, OMT or card once available.
  • Prices don’t include taxes, which you’re responsible for unless we say otherwise.
  • Premium renews every month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next charge, and you keep Premium until the end of the period you’ve paid for. After that the branch moves to the Free plan and nothing is deleted.
  • If an invoice goes unpaid, we pause the account and may later delete it. Late payment, pausing and deletion explains how.
  • Prices may change. We’ll tell you at least 30 days before a change applies to you.

No refunds

Every account can try Premium free before paying, so we don’t currently offer refunds. Fees you have paid aren’t refundable, including for partial months or unused time, unless the law says otherwise or we agree in writing. If this changes, we’ll update these terms.

No commission

We don’t take a commission on your orders. Our fee is the plan fee, nothing on top.

4. Late payment, pausing and deletion

This section applies to paying customers when a Premium invoice isn’t paid by its due date.

  1. Grace period (7 days). If an invoice isn’t paid on its due date, we remind you and give you 7 days to pay. Everything keeps working during this time.
  2. Paused and marked “payment due”. If it’s still unpaid after the 7 days, we may pause the account, or only the branches the invoice covers, and mark it “payment due”. While an account is paused, your storefront stops taking new orders and you can’t use the portal except to pay, contact us and ask for an export of your data. Your content is kept for now.
  3. Paying turns it back on. When you pay what you owe, we switch the account back on as soon as we confirm the payment, with your content as you left it.
  4. Deletion (after 2 months). If the invoice is still unpaid 60 days after its due date, we may permanently delete your content, including your menu, products, photos, customers, orders and settings. Deleted content can’t be recovered. We decide what to delete and when. We’ll send reminders, including a final notice before we delete, to the email address or WhatsApp number you gave us.

Even after deletion we may keep what the law requires or what we reasonably need, such as billing and payment records, records of our messages to you, security records, and aggregated, anonymized data that no longer identifies you. Deleting your content doesn’t cancel what you owe for the period before it.

Save your work. Your menu, photos and settings are the effort you’ve put into your storefront, and they’re what we delete if an invoice stays unpaid. You can ask for an export at any time. If you don’t want to keep paying, cancel Premium before it renews: the branch moves to the Free plan and nothing is deleted.

5. Online payments and tips

When online payments are available, each Merchant connects its own payment account with a payment provider. Lebanon comes first, and other countries will follow. Some points to know:

  • Dinelabs isn’t a bank or a payment provider. Your agreement for payments is with the provider, and its terms apply.
  • You give the provider accurate business, bank and ID details, and you’re responsible for keeping them current.
  • The provider decides whether to approve your account, and can refuse or end it.
  • Refunds, chargebacks and payment disputes with guests are between you, the guest and the provider. When you reject or cancel a paid card order, you decide whether to refund it, and we record that on the order.
  • Tips belong to you. You decide how to share them with your team.
  • Cash is always available as a payment method. Orders show whether they’re paid, unpaid (cash to collect) or failed.

  • refunds, complaints and any payment you collect from guests
  • getting any consent you need to contact your guests, and using their details only to serve their orders and as the law allows
  • following the food, health, consumer, tax and data protection laws that apply to your business

  • Menu import shows you a review screen. Nothing is saved until you approve it, and you’re responsible for what you approve.
  • Dinelabs isn’t responsible for mistakes in automatic translations, imports or enhanced pictures.
